Privacy policy

Huru app (com.huru.app) · Version 1 · 3 October 2026

In short: Huru works without an account and without a server. Everything you enter stays on your phone, in an encrypted database. We collect nothing, we share nothing, and no analytics or advertising tool is built in.

1. Who is responsible

Huru is published by Osée EBOUA EPANE, data controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). For any question: ebouaepane@gmail.com.

2. What Huru does

Huru helps you count your days without relapse. You choose what you are quitting and a goal in days, then tap “I held on” every day. The app computes your current streak, your record, your total and, if you entered it, the money you have saved.

3. The data processed, and where it lives

All the data below is stored only on your phone, in an encrypted database (SQLCipher). The encryption key is generated on the device and kept in the system’s secure store (Android Keystore). It is never sent to us.

  • your tracker: what you are quitting (or a free label), the start date and the goal in days;
  • your daily check-ins (“I held on”), catch-ups and declared relapses, with their dates;
  • the history of your attempts and the milestones you reached;
  • the daily cost and currency, if you enter them to see the money saved;
  • your settings: language, colour theme, daily reminder, lock, country for help resources.

Huru does not record any name, e-mail address, phone number, location or contacts.

4. What never leaves your phone

  • No account and no server: the app works offline.
  • No collection: we have no access to your data and cannot read it.
  • No sharing with third parties, no sale.
  • No usage analytics, no advertising identifier, no tracking or advertising tool built in.

The only possible exception concerns in-app purchases (see section 7): the payment is then processed by Google Play, not by Huru.

5. The permissions requested

  • Notifications: for the daily reminder, if you turn it on. The notification is created on your phone, with no server.
  • Biometrics: for the app lock, if you turn it on. Authentication is performed by the system; Huru never sees your fingerprint or your face, only the “passed” or “failed” result.
  • Device start-up: to reschedule the daily reminder after a reboot.

Huru does not request access to the Internet, your files, your location or your contacts.

6. Backup, export and deletion

  • The system’s automatic backup is disabled: the encrypted database is not part of the device’s Google backups, because its key cannot travel with them.
  • Uninstalling Huru permanently deletes all its data.
  • A future version will offer, from the settings, the export of your data (JSON or CSV) and their deletion in one step.

7. Purchases

This version of Huru contains no purchase. If a Premium offer is introduced in a future version, payment will go through Google Play in-app purchases: Google then processes the transaction under its own privacy policy, and Huru receives neither your payment details nor your identity.

8. Health

Huru is a personal support tool. It is not a medical device and the app gives no medical advice. If you need it, the “Need help” button on the home screen points you to emergency numbers and helplines for your country.

9. Age

Huru is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

10. Your rights

Your data is held by you alone, on your device. You can view it in the app, change it from the settings and delete it by uninstalling Huru (and directly from the app in a future version). We cannot access it on your behalf. For any question about this policy or to exercise a right under the GDPR, write to ebouaepane@gmail.com. You may also contact the data protection authority of your country.

11. Changes

This policy may evolve with the app. The version in force is always the one published at this address; significant changes will be announced in the app.
